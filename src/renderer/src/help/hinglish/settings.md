# Settings

[Settings](total://screen/simple-section?section=settings) woh jagah hai jahan aap business ko ek baar set karte hain, aur baad mein badalte hain.

- **Business details** — naam, pata, GST number, GST kitni baar file karte hain.
- **Pehle din ka hisaab** — dekhein [Pehle din ka hisaab](total://help/day-one).
- **Bills aur printing** — aapke bills kaise dikhte hain.
- **Backups** — dekhein [Backups](total://help/backups).
- **People aur unke adhikaar** — doosron ko unka apna sign-in dein: maalik, accountant jo entry likh aur badal sakta hai, ya viewer jo sirf dekh sakta hai.
- **Features** — jo aapke business ko chahiye use chalu karein, jaise stock, maal banana, salary ya TDS (source par kata hua tax): [Features](total://screen/simple-features).
- **AI helper** — optional: ek AI service jiska paisa aap alag se dete hain, apni key ke saath, woh jawab deti hai jo Total nahi de paata. Jab tak aap chalu nahi karte kuch nahi bheja jaata; screen batati hai kya bheja jaata hai aur kya chhupaya jaata hai: [AI helper](total://screen/simple-ai).
- **Total kaisa dikhe** — aasaan screens ya poora app, light ya dark, aur bhasha: [Total kaisa dikhe](total://screen/simple-looks).
- **Help aur tour** — Home ka tour dobara dekhein, ya yeh help kholein: [Help aur tour](total://screen/simple-help).
- **Advanced** — poore app ki har setting.

## Is computer par passphrase yaad rakhein

Agar business par passphrase hai, to use maangne wale box mein **Is computer par yaad rakhein** hota hai. Yeh kabhi apne-aap nahi chuna jaata. Chunne par hisaab yahan bina passphrase ke khulta hai. Total computer ke keychain mein ek chaabi rakhta hai, passphrase kabhi nahi. Jo bhi is computer par sign-in kar sakta hai, woh aapka hisaab khol sakega — isliye ise sirf aise computer par chunein jise sirf aap istemaal karte hain.

[Taala aur suraksha](total://screen/simple-security), business ki list aur screen ka taala "Is computer par yaad hai" batate hain, saath mein **Bhool jaayein**. Passphrase hatane par bhi yeh bhool jaata hai.
