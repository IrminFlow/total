# Shuruaat

Total aapke business ka hisaab isi computer par rakhta hai. Jo hota hai aap woh likhte hain — sale, purchase, paise aaye, paise gaye — aur Total hisaab lagata hai ki kisse lena hai, kisko dena hai, aapka profit kitna hai aur aapka GST (Goods and Services Tax) kitna hai.

## Aapka pehla din

1. Kuch bechein to **Bill banayein**: [Bill banayein](total://screen/simple-sell).
2. Koi supplier aapko bill de to **Purchase likhein**: [Purchase likhein](total://screen/simple-buy).
3. Koi aapko paise de to **Paise aaye**, aap kisi ko paise dein to **Paise gaye**: [Paise aaye](total://screen/simple-money-in), [Paise gaye](total://screen/simple-money-out).
4. Din ke end mein left side ke sabse neeche **Abhi backup lein** button se **backup lein**. Dekhein [Backup](total://help/backups).

## Home

Home par aaj ke paise aaye aur gaye, cash aur bank, kisse lena hai, kisko dena hai, aur — agar aap GST mein registered hain — sarkar ko kitna dena hoga aur kab tak, yeh sab dikhta hai. Har figure ke saath **Kyun?** hota hai, jo ek line mein batata hai ki woh kahan se aaya.

Home ke bade buttons (**+ Sale**, **+ Purchase**, **Paise aaye**, **Paise gaye**) woh chaar kaam hain jo aap sabse zyada karenge.

## Kya business pehle se chal raha tha?

Total ko batayein ki jis din aapne ise use karna shuru kiya, us din aapke paas kya tha — cash, bank, customers se kitna lena tha, suppliers ko kitna dena tha, aapka stock. Dekhein [Pehle din aapke paas kya tha](total://help/day-one).

## Koi galti ho gayi?

Bill, purchase, paise aaye ya gaye, ya wapsi save karne ke baad ek card dikhta hai ki Total ne kya likha, saath mein **Undo karein** hota hai. Undo ek din tak kaam karta hai, jab tak koi aur cheez us entry par tiki na ho. Uske baad entry kholein aur use badlein.

## Aur madad chahiye?

- Kisi bhi screen par us screen ki help kholne ke liye **?** ya **⌘/** dabayein.
- **⌘J** se Total se aasaan shabdon mein sawaal poochhein: "mujhe kisse paise lene hain?", "is mahine ka profit".
- Home ka chhota tour phir se dekhna hai? [Help aur tour](total://screen/simple-help) kholein.
