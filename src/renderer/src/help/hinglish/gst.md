# Aasaan shabdon mein GST

GST (Goods and Services Tax) woh tax hai jo aap apne bills mein jodte hain aur sarkar ko bharte hain. Total figures rakhta hai aur files taiyaar karta hai; **file aap karte hain** — aap (ya aapke CA) GST portal [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in) par file karte hain.

## GST mein registered nahi hain?

Tab aapke bills par GST nahi lagta aur file karne ko kuch nahi hai. Agar aap baad mein register hote hain, to ise Business ki jaankari mein badlein aur woh date dein jab se aap registered hain (aaj ki, jab tak aap use na badlein); Total us date se bills mein GST jodta hai.

## Registered (regular)

[Tax](total://screen/simple-taxes) har period ke liye teen alag figures dikhata hai:

1. **Aapne jo GST collect kiya** — aapki sale par.
2. **Purchase par diya GST** — suppliers ne aapse jo liya, jo aam taur par aapke bharne wale GST mein se kam ho jaata hai. Total yeh bhi batata hai ki ismein se kitna aapke suppliers ne abhi portal par confirm nahi kiya hai (unka GSTR-2B).
3. **Cash mein bharna hai** — cash mein kitna bharna baaki hai, aur kab tak.

## Quarterly filers

Agar aap har quarter file karte hain, to [Taxes](total://screen/simple-taxes) mein teesra step hai **Quarter ki GSTR-1 files taiyaar karein** (aur GSTR-3B). Yeh quarter ke teeno mahine ki files ek folder mein banata hai. Quarterly return ke liye unhe kaise upload karein, yeh apne CA se poochein. Quarter ke "GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B kholein" links uska aakhri mahina kholte hain, yaani woh mahina jiske liye return bhara jaata hai.

## E-invoicing

Agar aapne Business details mein apna turnover bhara hai (sirf maalik) aur woh e-invoicing ki seema se upar hai, to Taxes screen batati hai "E-invoicing aap par ek tareekh se laagu hai — ise chalu karein". Yeh sirf soochna hai: Total e-invoicing ko kabhi apne-aap chalu nahi karta.

## Dates

- **GSTR-1** (aapki sale): agle mahine ki 11 tareekh tak. Agar aap har quarter file karte hain, to quarter ke baad 13 tareekh tak (Notification 83/2020-Central Tax).
- **GSTR-3B** (summary aur payment): agle mahine ki 20 tareekh tak (CGST Rules ka rule 61). Agar aap har quarter file karte hain, to quarter ke baad 22 ya 24 tareekh tak, aapke state ke hisaab se.
- **Composition** wale business har quarter ke baad 18 tareekh tak CMP-08 file karte hain aur saal mein ek baar GSTR-4 (CGST Rules ka rule 62).

## Total kya nahi jaanta

- Total in rules mein likhi dates use karta hai. Sarkar kisi ek mahine ya ek state ke liye jo date badhati hai, uski jaankari Total ko nahi hoti.
- Quarterly file karne walon ke liye, har mahine ka payment (PMT-06, 25 tareekh tak) bataya jaata hai, par uska amount calculate nahi kiya jaata.
- Notification 83/2020 mein baad mein hue badlaav check nahi kiye gaye hain.

Shak ho to portal ya [CBIC](https://cbic-gst.gov.in) par dekh lein.

## File karna, step by step

Tax screen aapko har step par le chalti hai: check karein ki har bill likha gaya hai, suppliers ke bills portal se milayein, files taiyaar karein, unhe portal par file karein, phir **Maine file kar diya** dabayein aur portal se mila acknowledgement number (ARN) likhein.
