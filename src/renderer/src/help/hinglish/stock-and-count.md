# Stock aur ginti

Jab aap stock rakhte hain, to Total har product par nazar rakhta hai: aapne kya khareeda, kya becha, kya bacha hai aur uski keemat kitni hai.

## Products

[Stock → Products](total://screen/simple-stock) har product ki list dikhata hai — aapke paas kitna hai, uski keemat kitni hai aur aapka bechne ka daam. Jo products kam ho rahe hain unpar nishaan hota hai — [Kam ho raha hai](total://screen/simple-stock?filter=low) sirf wahi dikhata hai.

Naya product jodne ke liye **+ Naya product** dabayein: uska naam, unit (pieces, kg …), bechne ka daam aur, jab aap GST mein registered hon, uska GST rate.

Kisi product ko kholkar uska daam badlein ya uski history aasaan shabdon mein dekhein: khareeda, becha, wapas aaya, gina.

## Stock ginein

Beech-beech mein ginein ki shelf par asal mein kitna hai. [Stock ginein](total://screen/simple-stock-count) kholein, jo gina woh likhein, aur likhte-likhte Total farak dikhata jaata hai. Save karein, aur Total apni quantity theek kar leta hai.

## Kam hone par batayein

Koi product kholkar **Kab batayein** bharein — uske unit mein, kitne se kam hone par. Khaali chhodein ya 0 likhein to sirf tab bataya jaata hai jab woh khatam ho jaaye. Aise products [Kam ho raha hai](total://screen/simple-stock?filter=low) mein dikhte hain.

## History aur save ki hui ginti

Product ki history ki koi bhi line us entry ko kholti hai — bill, purchase, return ya ginti. Save ki hui ginti dikhati hai ki Total ke paas kitna tha, aapne kitna gina aur usse stock ki keemat par kya asar pada. Agar us stock mein se kuch baad mein bik gaya ya istemaal ho gaya, to ginti wapas nahi li ja sakti; entry badlein.

## Stock apne-aap badalta hai

Har bill, purchase aur return se stock apne-aap badalta hai. Ginti ki zaroorat sirf un cheezon ke liye hai jo kho gayi, toot gayi ya galat gini gayi.

Kachche maal se apna maal banate hain? Dekhein [Taiyaar maal](total://help/made-goods).
