# Accountant view aur aapke CA

Total ke do roop hain: aasaan screens, jinhe aap roz istemaal karte hain, aur **poora app**, jise aapke accountant (CA) jaante hain — har screen aur har report, hisaab-kitaab ki bhasha mein. Dono ek hi hisaab par kaam karte hain; kuch bhi copy ya convert nahi karna padta.

## Ek screen par Accountant view

Jin aasaan screens par aap kuch likhte ya jaanchte hain, unmein se zyadatar par **Accountant view** button hota hai. Yeh usi kaam ki poori screen kholta hai — bill ka poora form, bank ka poora milan, GST ka poora return. **Aasaan screen par laut-ein** se aap wapas aa jaate hain.

Uske paas, **Iske liye hamesha poora app kholein** us screen ke liye aapki pasand yaad rakhta hai.

## Poora business badlein

Maalik poore business ko [Total kaisa dikhe](total://screen/simple-looks) mein poore app par badal sakte hain. Wahan se aasaan screens bhi bas ek click door rehti hain.

## Apne CA ko hisaab dena

- [Reports → Aapke CA ke liye](total://screen/simple-section?section=reports) mein woh sab reports hain jo aapke accountant maangte hain, aur Excel mein export bhi.
- Unhe ek encrypted backup dein ([Settings → Backups](total://screen/settings?tab=backups) mein **Export encrypted…**, sirf maalik) aur uska passphrase — woh use Total ki apni copy mein kholte hain.
- Agar woh isi computer par kaam karte hain, to unhe [Settings → People aur unke adhikaar](total://screen/settings?tab=users) mein unka apna sign-in dein.

## Aasaan screen par lautne par aapke badlaav saath aate hain

Aasaan form se poora editor khola, wahan kuch badla (quantity, customer, note) aur bina save kiye **Aasaan screen par laut-ein** dabaya? Aasaan form aapke badlaav dikhata hai, aur kuch chhodne ke baare mein nahi poochta.

Agar poore editor mein kuch aisa tha jiski aasaan form mein jagah nahi — entry number, TDS, dhulai ki jaankari — to ek note use ginata hai aur tab tak rehta hai jab tak aap use band na karein ya save na karein. Unhe poore app mein save karein. Agar jawab bilkul saath nahi aa sake, to poora form khaali khulta hai aur batata hai: "Kuch jawab saath nahi aa sake".
