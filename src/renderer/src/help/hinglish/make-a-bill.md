# Bill banayein

Jab bhi aap kuch bechein, **Bill banayein** use karein — dukaan par aaye aise customer ke liye jo abhi paise de raha hai, ya aise customer ke liye jo baad mein dega. Ise Home par **+ Sale** se ya [Bechein → Bill banayein](total://screen/simple-sell) se kholein.

## Step by step

1. **Bill kiske liye hai?** Dukaan par aaye customer ke liye, jo abhi paise de raha hai, ise cash customer hi rehne dein, ya kisi customer ka naam likhein. Naya naam wahin jod sakte hain.
2. **Bill ki date** — aaj ki, jab tak aap koi purana bill na likh rahe hon.
3. **Kya becha?** Items chunein aur kitne. Total aapka bechne ka rate bhar deta hai. Items nahi hain? **Sirf amount** chunein aur total amount likhein.
4. **Payment kaise hua?** Abhi cash, UPI, card ya bank transfer — ya baad mein, **udhaar**. Agar customer sirf thode paise deta hai, to **Thoda paid** chunein aur likhein ki usne kitna diya; baaki udhaar rehta hai. Cash customer hamesha poore paise deta hai — udhaar par bechne ke liye customer chunein.
5. **Save karein** dabayein (ya **⌘↵**).

## Save karne ke baad

Ek card aasaan shabdon mein dikhata hai ki Total ne kya likha, saath mein **Print karein**, **PDF** aur **Share karein**. Agar galti se save ho gaya ho, to **Undo karein** bill ko hata deta hai.

## Bill par GST

Agar aapka business GST mein registered hai, to Total har item ke rate se GST ka hisaab lagata hai aur tax invoice print karta hai. Composition wala business bill of supply print karta hai. Agar aap registered nahi hain, to bill par GST nahi hota aur woh invoice ki tarah print hota hai.

## Quick bill

Bheed wale counter par [Quick bill](total://screen/simple-quick-bill) use karein: ek hi screen par items scan karein ya likhein aur paise lein.

## Apne bill dekhein

[Sales ki list](total://screen/simple-sales) har bill dikhati hai, saath mein kitna paid hua aur kitna abhi baaki hai. Kisi bill ko kholkar use phir se print karein, payment likhein ya wapsi shuru karein.
