# Taiyaar maal

Agar aap kachche maal se cheezein banate hain — aate se bread, doodh aur cheeni se mithai ka dabba — to har baar jitna banaya, use **Taiyaar maal** mein likhein. Total kachcha maal aapke stock se ghatata hai aur taiyaar maal jodta hai, usi lagat par jo asal mein aapko padi.

Yeh screen tabhi dikhti hai jab [Features](total://screen/simple-features) mein **Maal banana** chalu ho.

## Step by step

1. [Stock → Taiyaar maal](total://screen/simple-made-goods) kholein.
2. Woh product chunein jo aapne banaya. Total dikhata hai ki uska ek piece kin cheezon se banta hai.
3. Likhein ki aapne kitne banaye. Total dikhata hai ki isme kitna kachcha maal lagega — aur jo cheez aapke paas poori nahi hai.
4. Agar aap lagat mein mazdoori jodna chahein, to mazdoori ka kharcha likhein.
5. **Save karein** dabayein.

## Ek product kin cheezon se banta hai

Ek product mein kya-kya lagta hai, woh product ke Recipe hisse mein hai, neeche dekhein. Pehle ki recipes poore app mein hain: dekhein [Accountant view aur aapke CA](total://help/accountant-view).

## Product par recipe

**Maal banana** chalu ho to [Stock → Products](total://screen/simple-stock) mein koi product kholein. Uske **Recipe** hisse mein likha hota hai "1 Bread banane mein … lagta hai". **Recipe jodein** (ya **Recipe badlein**) dabayein, har kachcha maal aur ek piece mein kitna lagta hai chunein, aur chahein to har piece ki mazdoori bhi. Save karein.

Badli hui recipe aapke kaam ki tareekh se laagu hoti hai; pehle jo banaya uski purani recipe hi rehti hai. Koi product apne hi andar nahi ja sakta. Taiyaar maal jitne banaye utni mazdoori khud bhar deta hai, aur aap use wahan badal sakte hain.
