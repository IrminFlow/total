# Paise aaye aur paise gaye

Jo bhi rupaya aata hai ya jaata hai, woh yahan likha jaata hai, taaki aapke cash aur bank ke figures sahi rahein.

## Paise aaye

[Paise aaye](total://screen/simple-money-in) tab kholein jab:

- koi customer aapko paise de — customer chunein aur Total pehle uske sabse purane bina chukaye bills par tick lagata hai (kaun-se bills, yeh aap badal sakte hain);
- aap apna paisa business mein daalein;
- aap loan lein;
- aapko koi doosri income ho, jaise byaaj;
- koi supplier aapko refund de.

Batayein ki paisa cash mein aaya ya kisi bank account mein.

## Paise gaye

[Paise gaye](total://screen/simple-money-out) tab kholein jab:

- aap kisi supplier ko payment karein — uske bina chukaye bills dikhte hain aur sabse purane pehle tick hote hain;
- aap koi kharcha chukayein — kiraya, salary, chai, bijli. Tiles mein se chunein ki yeh kis cheez ke liye tha;
- aap apne liye paise nikaalein;
- aap salary dein (jab “Features” mein salary chalu ho).

Loan lautana abhi choices mein nahi hai — apne accountant se kahein ki woh ise full app mein likhein.

## Bill se pehle paise mil gaye?

Agar koi customer uska bill banane se pehle hi paise de de, to "Kya yeh aise bill ke liye hai jo aapne abhi nahi banaya?" ka jawaab **Haan** dein. Total ise advance ki tarah rakhta hai aur uske agle bills mein se kaat deta hai.

## Paise transfer karein

[Paise transfer karein](total://screen/simple-move-money) aapke apne cash aur bank accounts ke beech paisa le jaane ke liye hai — cash bank mein jama karna, ya ATM se cash nikaalna. Yeh na income hai, na kharcha.

## Sab dekhein

[Paise aaye aur gaye](total://screen/simple-money) har entry ki list dikhata hai. Kisi ko kholkar aasaan shabdon mein dekhein, ya poore form mein badalne ke liye **Edit karein** dabayein. Galti se save ho gaya? Save karne ke turant baad dikhne wale card par **Undo karein** hai.
