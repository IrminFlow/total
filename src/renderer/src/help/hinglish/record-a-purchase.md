# Purchase likhein

Jab koi supplier aapko bill de, tab **Purchase likhein** use karein — us maal ke liye jo aap bechenge, ya kiraya ya bijli jaise kisi kharche ke liye. Ise Home par **+ Purchase** se ya [Kharidein → Purchase likhein](total://screen/simple-buy) se kholein.

## Step by step

1. **Supplier** — unka naam likhein. Naya supplier wahin jod sakte hain.
2. **Unka bill number aur date** — inhe bill se copy karein. Total ek hi supplier ka ek hi bill number saal mein do baar nahi leta, taaki kuch bhi do baar na likha jaye.
3. **Aapne kya kharida?** Items aur kitne, ya kisi kharche ke liye **Sirf amount**.
4. **Bill par GST** — jab aap GST mein registered hon, to Total aapke diye GST ka hisaab lagata hai; file karte waqt yeh aapke bharne wale GST mein se kam ho jata hai. Agar aapka business ise wapas claim nahi kar sakta, to Total ise cost mein jod deta hai.
5. **Abhi payment kiya?** Agar aapne kharidte waqt paise diye, to batayein kaise. Nahi to bill supplier ka baaki rehta hai.
6. **Save karein** dabayein.

## Supplier ko baad mein payment

Jab aap payment karein, to [Paise gaye](total://screen/simple-money-out) use karein aur supplier chunein — Total unke bina chukaye bill dikhata hai aur jo aap chuka rahe hain un par tick lagata hai.

## Apni purchase dekhein

[Purchase ki list](total://screen/simple-purchases) har purchase bill dikhati hai, saath mein kitna paid hua aur kitna abhi baaki hai. Maal wapas bheja? Dekhein [Wapsi](total://help/returns).
