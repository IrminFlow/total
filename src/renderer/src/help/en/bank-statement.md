# Bank statement

Import your bank statement and Total matches it to what you recorded. Anything the bank shows that you have not recorded yet is listed with the question **What is this?**

## Step by step

1. Open [Money → Bank](total://screen/simple-bank). No bank account yet? Press **Add a bank account** first.
2. Drop in the statement file from your bank's website or app — CSV or Excel (MT940 and CAMT files work too). PDF statements cannot be read yet. A line you added before is never added twice.
3. For each new line Total suggests what it is — a customer paying, a supplier you paid, bank charges — and says why ("because …"). Check it, change it if needed, and press **Save**. **Save all** saves every line that has an answer.
4. Lines you had already recorded are matched by themselves and shown as done.

## Not sure yet? Wrong match?

- **Skip for now** leaves a line for later. Skipped lines wait under **Skipped for now** (remembered on this computer) with **Bring back**.
- A line marked done has **Not this** (accountants and owners). The line goes back to the lines to answer; the entry stays in your books. If that entry was wrong, open it and remove it.

## Money paid out, and bank charges

If the money went on an expense, Total asks "Do you have a GST bill?", as Money out does. For **Bank charges**, tick **The charge includes GST** so the GST counts toward what you claim back.

## The sentence at the top

Total compares your books with the bank. When they agree it says so. When they differ it says how many entries differ — usually entries in your books the bank has not shown yet, such as cheques that have not cleared — and **see them** opens the full matching screen.
