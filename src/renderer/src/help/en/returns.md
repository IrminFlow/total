# Returns

When goods come back from a customer — or you send goods back to a supplier — record a return, so your sales, your stock and what is owed all go down by the right amount.

## A customer returned goods

1. Open [Return a sale](total://screen/simple-return-sale), or open the bill in the [Sales list](total://screen/simple-sales) and start the return from there.
2. Pick the bill. Total shows what is left to return on it.
3. Say what came back, and how many.
4. **Money back now, or keep it as credit?** If the bill was unpaid, the return simply lowers what the customer owes. If they had paid, give the money back or keep it for their next bill.
5. Press **Save**.

## You returned goods to a supplier

Use [Return a purchase](total://screen/simple-return-purchase) the same way: pick their bill, say what went back, and whether they will refund you or take it off their next bill.

## GST on a return

The GST on a return is taken from the original bill, at the same rate — so the GST you collected (or paid) goes down by exactly the right amount.
