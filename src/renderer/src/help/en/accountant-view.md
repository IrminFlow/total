# Accountant view and your CA

Total has two faces: the simple screens you use every day, and the **full app** your accountant (CA) knows — every screen and report, in accounting terms. Both work on the same books; nothing has to be copied or converted.

## Accountant view on one screen

Every simple screen has an **Accountant view** button. It opens the full screen for the same thing — the full bill form, the full bank matching, the full GST return. **Back to simple** brings you back.

Next to it, **Always open the full app for this** remembers your choice for that screen.

## Switch the whole business

The owner can switch the whole business to the full app in [How Total looks](total://screen/simple-looks). The simple screens are one click away there too.

## Handing the books to your CA

- [Reports → For your CA](total://screen/simple-section?section=reports) has every report your accountant asks for, and an export to Excel.
- Give them a backup — they can open it in their own copy of Total.
- If they work on this computer, give them their own sign-in in [Settings → People & roles](total://screen/settings?tab=users).
