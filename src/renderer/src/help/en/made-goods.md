# Made goods

If you make products from raw material — bread from flour, a box of sweets from milk and sugar — record each batch in **Made goods**. Total takes the raw material off your stock and adds the finished products, at what they really cost you.

This screen shows only while **Making goods** is turned on in [Features](total://screen/simple-features).

## Step by step

1. Open [Stock → Made goods](total://screen/simple-made-goods).
2. Pick the product you made. Total shows what one of it is made from.
3. Type how many you made. Total shows the raw material it will use — and anything you do not have enough of.
4. Add the cost of labour, if you want it in the cost.
5. Press **Save**.

## What one product is made from

The list of what goes into one product is set once, in the full app. Ask your accountant, or see [Accountant view and your CA](total://help/accountant-view).
