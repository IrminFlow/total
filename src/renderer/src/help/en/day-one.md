# What you had on day one

If your business was already running before you started using Total, tell Total what you had on that day. Then every figure — cash, bank, who owes you, your stock — starts right.

Open [What you had on day one](total://screen/simple-openings) from Home or from Settings.

## What it asks

1. **Money** — cash in hand, and what was in each bank account. A bank account you owed money on (an overdraft) goes under what you owe.
2. **Customers who owed you** — each customer's amount, and their bills if you want them followed one by one.
3. **Suppliers you owed** — the same, the other way round.
4. **Stock** — how many of each product, at what cost (only when you keep stock).
5. **Loans and things you own** — a loan you took or gave, and things the business has used for years, such as a shop counter or a vehicle.

## The owner's money

On the last step Total works out what is left as the owner's money in the business, and says it in one sentence. Check it and press **Save**.

## Started fresh?

If the business started with nothing, press **I started fresh** and Total stops asking.
