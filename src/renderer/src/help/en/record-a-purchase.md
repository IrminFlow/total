# Record a purchase

Use **Record a purchase** when a supplier gives you a bill — for goods you will sell, or for an expense such as rent or electricity. Open it from **+ Purchase** on Home or from [Buy → Record a purchase](total://screen/simple-buy).

## Step by step

1. **Supplier** — type their name. A new supplier can be added on the spot.
2. **Their bill number and date** — copy them from the bill. Total refuses the same bill number from the same supplier twice in a year, so nothing is entered twice.
3. **What did you buy?** Products and how many, or **Just an amount** for an expense.
4. **GST on the bill** — when you are registered for GST, Total works out the GST you paid; it comes off the GST you owe when you file. If your business cannot claim it back, Total adds it to the cost.
5. **Paid now?** If you paid when you bought, say how. Otherwise the bill stays due to the supplier.
6. Press **Save**.

## Paying the supplier later

When you pay, use [Money out](total://screen/simple-money-out) and pick the supplier — Total shows their unpaid bills and ticks the ones you pay.

## See your purchases

The [Purchase list](total://screen/simple-purchases) shows every purchase bill with what is paid and what is still due. Sent goods back? See [Returns](total://help/returns).
