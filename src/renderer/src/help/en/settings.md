# Settings

[Settings](total://screen/simple-section?section=settings) is where you set up the business once, and change it later.

- **Business details** — name, address, GST number, how often you file GST.
- **What you had on day one** — see [What you had on day one](total://help/day-one).
- **Bills & printing** — what your bills look like.
- **Backups** — see [Backups](total://help/backups).
- **People & roles** — give others their own sign-in: an owner, an accountant who can enter and change things, or a viewer who can only look.
- **Features** — turn on what your business needs, such as stock, making goods, salaries or TDS (tax deducted at source): [Features](total://screen/simple-features).
- **AI helper** — optional: an AI service you pay for separately, with your own key, answers what Total can’t. Nothing is sent until you turn it on; the screen says what is sent and what is hidden: [AI helper](total://screen/simple-ai).
- **How Total looks** — simple screens or the full app, light or dark, and the language: [How Total looks](total://screen/simple-looks).
- **Help & tour** — show the tour of Home again, or open this help: [Help & tour](total://screen/simple-help).
- **Advanced** — every setting of the full app.

## Remember the passphrase on this computer

If the business has a passphrase, the box that asks for it has **Remember on this computer**. It is never ticked for you. Ticked, the books open here without the passphrase. Only the owner can do this: where the business has sign-ins, it is kept only if the owner signs in at that open. Total keeps a key in the computer's keychain, never the passphrase itself. Anyone who can sign in to this computer can then open your books, so tick it only on a computer that only you use.

[Lock and safety](total://screen/simple-security), the business list and the lock screen say "Remembered on this computer" with **Forget**. Taking the passphrase off forgets it too.
