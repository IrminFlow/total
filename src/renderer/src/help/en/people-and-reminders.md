# People and reminders

[People](total://screen/simple-people) is your list of customers and suppliers — with phone numbers, how many days they get to pay, and where they stand today.

## Who owes you

The list shows what each customer still owes you and how late it is. Show only [those who owe you](total://screen/simple-people?filter=owesYou) or only [late payers](total://screen/simple-people?filter=late).

## A person's page

Open anyone to see:

- their phone and WhatsApp numbers and days to pay;
- their unpaid bills, oldest first;
- buttons to record money in or out, make a bill or record a purchase;
- **Statement** — every bill and payment this year, as a PDF you can send;
- **Send a reminder** — a polite message by WhatsApp or SMS, or copy it. Total notes when you sent it.

## Bills to pay

[Bills to pay](total://screen/simple-people?tab=bills) lists every supplier bill that is still unpaid, with its due date. **Pay** opens Money out for that bill.

## A customer who does not pay

The owner can put a customer **on hold**: while they are on hold, new bills on credit to them are refused.
