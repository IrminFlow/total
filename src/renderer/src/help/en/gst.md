# GST in plain words

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is the tax you add to your bills and pay to the government. Total keeps the figures and prepares the files; **you file** — you (or your CA) file on the GST portal, [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in).

## Not registered for GST?

Then your bills carry no GST and there is nothing to file. If you register later, change it in Business details and Total starts adding GST from that date.

## Registered (regular)

[Taxes](total://screen/simple-taxes) shows three separate figures for each period:

1. **GST you collected** on your sales.
2. **GST on your purchases** — what suppliers charged you, which usually comes off what you owe. Total also says how much of it your suppliers have not yet confirmed on the portal (their GSTR-2B).
3. **To pay** — what is left to pay in cash, and by when.

## The dates

- **GSTR-1** (your sales): by the 11th of the next month. If you file every quarter, by the 13th after the quarter (Notification 83/2020-Central Tax).
- **GSTR-3B** (the summary and the payment): by the 20th of the next month (rule 61 of the CGST Rules). If you file every quarter, by the 22nd or the 24th after the quarter, depending on your state.
- **Composition** businesses file CMP-08 by the 18th after each quarter and GSTR-4 once a year (rule 62 of the CGST Rules).

## What Total does not know

- Total uses the dates in those rules. It does not know about extensions the government announces for one month or one state.
- For quarterly filers, the monthly payment (PMT-06, by the 25th) is named but its amount is not worked out.
- Changes to Notification 83/2020 made later have not been checked.

When in doubt, check the portal or [CBIC](https://cbic-gst.gov.in).

## Filing, step by step

The Taxes screen walks you through it: check that every bill is in, check supplier bills against the portal, prepare the files, file them on the portal, then press **I've filed** and type the reference number (ARN) the portal gives you.
