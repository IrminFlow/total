# Make a bill

Use **Make a bill** whenever you sell something — to a walk-in customer who pays now, or to a customer who will pay later. Open it from **+ Sale** on Home or from [Sell → Make a bill](total://screen/simple-sell).

## Step by step

1. **Who is it for?** Leave it as the cash customer for a walk-in who pays now, or type a customer's name. A new name can be added on the spot.
2. **Bill date** — today, unless you are entering an older bill.
3. **What was sold?** Pick the products and how many. Total fills in the price you sell at. No products? Choose **Just an amount** and type the total.
4. **Paid how?** Cash, UPI, card or bank transfer now — or later, **on credit (udhaar)**. If the customer pays only part, choose **Partly paid** and type what they paid; the rest stays due. A cash customer always pays in full — to sell on credit, pick the customer.
5. Press **Save** (or **⌘↵**).

## After you save

A card shows what Total recorded, in plain words, with **Print**, **PDF** and **Share**. **Undo** takes the bill back out if you saved it by mistake.

## GST on the bill

If your business is registered for GST, Total works out the GST from each product's rate and prints a tax invoice. A composition business prints a bill of supply. If you are not registered, the bill has no GST and prints as an invoice.

## Quick bill

At a busy counter use [Quick bill](total://screen/simple-quick-bill): scan or type the products and take the money on one screen.

## See your bills

The [Sales list](total://screen/simple-sales) shows every bill with what was paid and what is still due. Open a bill to print it again, record a payment or start a return.
