# Getting started

Total keeps your business books on this computer. You record what happens — a sale, a purchase, money in, money out — and Total works out who owes you, what you owe, your profit and your GST (Goods and Services Tax).

## Your first day

1. **Make a bill** when you sell something: [Make a bill](total://screen/simple-sell).
2. **Record a purchase** when a supplier gives you a bill: [Record a purchase](total://screen/simple-buy).
3. **Money in** when someone pays you, **Money out** when you pay someone: [Money in](total://screen/simple-money-in), [Money out](total://screen/simple-money-out).
4. **Back up** at the end of the day with the **Back up now** button at the foot of the left side. See [Backups](total://help/backups).

## Home

Home shows today's money in and out, cash and bank, who owes you, whom you owe and — when you are registered for GST — what you will owe the government and by when. Every figure has a **Why?** that says where it comes from in one sentence.

The big buttons on Home (**+ Sale**, **+ Purchase**, **Money in**, **Money out**) are the four things you will do most.

## Was the business already running?

Tell Total what you had on the day you started using it — cash, bank, what customers owed you, what you owed suppliers, your stock. See [What you had on day one](total://help/day-one).

## Made a mistake?

Every save shows a card with **Undo**. Undo works for a day, as long as nothing else depends on the entry. After that, open the entry and change it.

## Need more?

- Press **?** or **⌘/** on any screen to open the help for that screen.
- Ask Total a question in plain words with **⌘J**: "who owes me?", "profit this month".
- Want the short tour of Home again? Open [Help & tour](total://screen/simple-help).
