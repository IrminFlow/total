# Reports

[Reports](total://screen/simple-section?section=reports) answer the questions every business owner asks, in plain words. Every figure comes from the same calculation the full reports use, so your accountant sees the same numbers.

## How is my business doing?

Sales, what the goods cost you, your expenses and your profit — for this month, last month, this year or dates you choose: [How is my business doing](total://screen/simple-reports). Each line has a **Why?**.

## Cash in the next 30 days

What should come in from customers and go out to suppliers over the next month, from the due dates on your bills: [Cash in the next 30 days](total://screen/simple-reports?report=cash).

## Who owes me / Whom I owe

Everyone with an unpaid balance, as on today: [Who owes me](total://screen/simple-reports?report=owed&side=receivable), [Whom I owe](total://screen/simple-reports?report=owed&side=payable).

## Sales and purchases by month

Month by month, before GST: [By month](total://screen/simple-reports?report=by-month).

## For your CA

At the bottom of [Reports](total://screen/simple-section?section=reports) are the reports your accountant asks for — the balance of every account, what the business owns and owes, profit and loss, cash flow — and an export of the whole books to Excel.
