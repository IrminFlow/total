# Money in and money out

Every rupee that comes in or goes out is recorded here, so your cash and bank figures stay right.

## Money in

Open [Money in](total://screen/simple-money-in) when:

- a customer pays you — pick the customer and Total ticks their oldest unpaid bills first (you can change which ones);
- you put your own money into the business;
- you take a loan;
- you get other income, such as interest;
- a supplier refunds you.

Say whether it came as cash or into a bank account.

## Money out

Open [Money out](total://screen/simple-money-out) when:

- you pay a supplier — their unpaid bills are shown and ticked oldest first;
- you pay an expense — rent, salary, tea, electricity. Pick what it was for from the tiles;
- you take money out for yourself;
- you pay salaries (when salaries are turned on in Features).

Repaying a loan is not one of the choices yet — ask your accountant to record it in the full app.

## Buying goods? Refunds?

Buying goods is not an expense. On Money out, **Buying goods? Record a purchase instead** opens [Record a purchase](total://help/record-a-purchase) with what you typed.

To pay a customer back money of theirs you hold (an advance, or a return kept for the next bill), pick **A customer (refund)** on Money out. If a supplier owes you nothing back, Money in says so and offers **Record it as other money in**.

## Paid before the bill?

If a customer pays before you make their bill, answer **Yes** to "Is this for a bill you haven't made yet?". Total keeps it as an advance and takes it off their next bills.

## Move money

[Move money](total://screen/simple-move-money) is for money moving between your own cash and bank accounts — putting cash in the bank, or taking cash out at an ATM. It is not income and not an expense.

## See it all

[Money in and out](total://screen/simple-money) lists every entry. Open one to see it in plain words, or press **Edit** to change it in the full form. Saved one by mistake? **Undo** is on the card that shows right after you save.
