# Backups

Your books live only on this computer, in your Documents folder. A backup is a copy you can go back to if the computer breaks or is lost, or if something was entered wrongly.

## Back up now

Press **Back up now** at the foot of the left side. It takes a second. Do it at the end of each working day.

Home reminds you when your last backup is more than a week old.

## Keep a copy somewhere else

A backup on the same computer does not help if the computer is lost. Now and then, copy your backups to a pen drive, or to a cloud folder you trust. [Settings → Backups](total://screen/settings?tab=backups) shows where they are kept.

## Bringing a backup back

Open the business, go to [Settings → Backups](total://screen/settings?tab=backups), pick the backup in the list and press **Restore…**. The books go back to how they were when that backup was made.

A backup saved with a passphrase (**Export encrypted…** in Settings → Backups) can also be opened on another computer: on the screen where you pick a business, choose **Import encrypted backup…** and type the passphrase.

## Where Total keeps your books

[Settings → Backups](total://screen/settings?tab=backups) shows the folder that holds your books and backups. If it is inside OneDrive or another cloud-sync folder, Total warns you, because a sync program can interfere with the books.

To change it, press **Move to another folder…** and pick an empty folder. Total copies everything, checks the copy and restarts. (If the folder you pick already holds Total books, Total opens those and copies nothing over them.) The old folder is not deleted, so do not go back to it — its books are older. Where the business has sign-ins, only the owner can move it.
