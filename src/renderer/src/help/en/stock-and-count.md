# Stock and counting

When you keep stock, Total follows every product: what you bought, what you sold, what is left and what it is worth.

## Products

[Stock → Products](total://screen/simple-stock) lists every product with how many you have, what they are worth and your selling price. Products running low are marked — [Running low](total://screen/simple-stock?filter=low) shows only those.

Press **+ New product** to add one: its name, unit (pieces, kg …), selling price and, when you are registered for GST, its GST rate.

Open a product to change its price or see its history in plain words: bought, sold, returned, counted.

## Count your stock

Now and then, count what is really on the shelf. Open [Count your stock](total://screen/simple-stock-count), type what you counted, and Total shows the difference as you type. Save, and Total corrects its quantity.

## Stock moves by itself

Every bill, purchase and return moves stock automatically. A count is only needed for things that went missing, broke or were miscounted.

Making your own goods from raw material? See [Made goods](total://help/made-goods).

## Tell me when it runs low

Open a product and fill in **Warn me when** — below how many, in its unit. Leave it blank or 0 to be told only when it runs out. Those products show under [Running low](total://screen/simple-stock?filter=low).

## History and saved counts

A line in a product's history opens that entry — the bill, the purchase, the return or the count. A saved count shows how many Total had, what you counted and what that did to the stock's worth. Undo of a count is refused if some of that stock was sold or used since; edit the entry instead.
