# Founding commercial policy

Effective policy date: 24 August 2026. Paid sales are not open yet.

## Public beta

Total v0.5 is free during the public beta. No card is collected and no beta installation is
automatically converted to a paid plan. Paid sales start only after at least 60 days' notice.

| Business | ₹9,900 + applicable taxes | One legal business, up to three devices | Perpetual use of the purchased major version; 12 months of updates and standard support |

Practice, plus applicable taxes. Care is not required to keep using the purchased major version.

state.

## Direct-purchase refund policy

A directly purchased licence can be refunded on request within 30 calendar days. Irmin Labs may
first offer reasonable help to fix the issue. Delivered services and reseller or marketplace
purchases follow the terms shown at that checkout, subject to applicable law.

## Support response targets

Support hours are Monday–Friday, 10:00–18:00 IST, excluding published holidays.

- Suspected data-loss or security incident: acknowledge within 4 business hours.
- Blocked core accounting workflow: acknowledge within 1 business day.
- Normal question, defect or product feedback: acknowledge within 2 business days.

These are operating targets, not guaranteed resolution times. Qualified legal review remains a
release gate before significant paid marketing or direct sales.
