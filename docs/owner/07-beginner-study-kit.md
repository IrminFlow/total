# Total: the real beginner study kit

**16 October 2026.** For the owner. This is plan decision D5: watch 5 or more first-time users per
business type use Total. A scripted stand-in study already ran (`docs/beginner-study-2026-10.md`);
it finds dead ends but cannot feel confused. Real people can. Claude prepared this kit; **you
recruit and run the sessions**, or hand it to a friend who can sit quietly.

Five business types, 5 people each, so **25 sessions at 30 to 40 minutes**. If that is too many,
start with 3 per type (15 sessions): most problems show up by the third person.

---

## 1. Who to recruit

- **Run a small business or help run one** of the type below. Not an accountant, not a developer.
- **Never used Total.** Has not seen it demoed.
- **Does not use Tally daily.** A mix is fine, but at least 3 of every 5 should not.
- **Mix of ages and comfort with computers.** At least one per type who finds computers hard.
- **Language:** offer English, Hindi or Hinglish. Ask which they read most easily. Run at least 1 per type in Hindi or Hinglish.
- **Not a friend who will be polite.** Ask them to be blunt.

| Type in the app | Typical person |
|---|---|
| Shop (kirana / retail) | Corner store, clothes, mobile shop |
| Trader / wholesaler | Sells to shops on udhaar |
| Services / freelancer | Designer, tutor, repairs |
| I make goods (manufacturer) | Small factory, namkeen, workshop |
| Restaurant / food | Dhaba, cafe, sweet shop |

### Invitation text (5 lines)

> Hi! I built an accounting app for small shops and need an honest first reaction.
> It takes 30 to 40 minutes: I give you a few simple jobs (make a bill, record a payment) and watch.
> You are not being tested. If something is confusing, that is my mistake, not yours.
> It uses made-up data on a test computer, nothing real of yours.
> Can you spare the time this week? I will bring tea.

---

## 2. Before each session

- A computer with Total installed and **nothing set up yet** (first screen is the language and setup wizard). Use a fresh data folder each time.
- Have a **bank statement file** ready for the bank task (ask Claude for the test file).
- Screen recording on (with consent, section 7). A stopwatch. This sheet, printed.
- Sit **beside** the person, not behind. Hands off the mouse.

---

## 3. Facilitator script

**Say, word for word at the start:**

> "Thank you. I am testing the app, not you. Nothing you do can be wrong. Please think aloud: say what you expect, what you are looking at, what confuses you. I will not help while you work, because I want to see what the app does without me. If you are completely stuck I will give a small hint and write it down. You can stop any time."

**While they work:**

- **Read the task once.** Repeat only if asked. Never rephrase it with app words.
- **Stay quiet.** Count to 10 before saying anything. Say "What are you thinking?" or "What would you do next?"
- **If they ask "is this right?"** say "What do you think?"
- **If they are stuck for 2 minutes,** give hint level 1 ("Look at the whole screen once more"). After another minute, hint level 2 (name the area: "the bottom of the screen"). Write down every hint.
- **Write down** every "huh?", every pause over 5 seconds, every wrong click, every error message that appears.

**Never say:**

- Any of these words: **ledger, voucher, journal, contra, debit, credit note (as a screen name), group, particulars, narration**. Say "bill", "who owes you", "money in", "money out".
- The names of buttons or screens ("click Save bill", "go to Money"). Describe the goal instead.
- "Good", "correct", "no, not that". Say "Thank you" after every task, whatever happened.
- "The app is supposed to…" or any excuse for it.

**At the end,** ask three questions: *What was the most confusing moment? What did you expect that was missing? Would you use this for your own shop, and what would stop you?* Write their words down.

## 4. The tasks

Read the sentence in the first column. Sessions run in this order. Tasks marked (S) need stock turned on, (G) need GST registration. "End on" is the screen the person should reach. **Done** is the test for "done unaided".

The "Watch for" column is what the scripted study found, so you know where real people may trip too.

### 4.1 Everyone does these

| # | Read aloud | End on | Done when | Watch for (from the proxy study) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Set up the app for your own kind of business. Use a made-up shop name." | Home, with the short tour offered | Business made; Home visible | Not sure about the GST question: "Not sure" is allowed. Choice of language first. |
| 2 | "Your customer Sharma bought two things for cash today. Make a bill for it." (S: use products. Services: use "just an amount".) | The save card ("Saved.") | Bill saved; total looks right to them | Adding a bank account when paying by UPI or card; whether they find **+ Sale**. |
| 3 | "Print that bill." | Print preview open | A page with the business name and amount appears | Looking for Print on the wrong screen. |
| 4 | "Another customer will pay next month. Make a bill that is not paid yet." | Save card; the line under the total shows when it is due | Saved as On credit (udhaar) with a named customer | Typing a near-duplicate name ("Sharmaji Store" next to "Sharma Stores"): does the "Did you mean?" help or confuse? |
| 5 | "A customer paid you some of what they owe. Record it." | Money in saved | The right customer owes less | Choosing the wrong customer; the idea of "oldest bill first". |
| 6 | "You paid the electricity bill from the shop's cash. Record it." | Money out saved | Saved as an expense | Asking for a supplier name when it is a cash expense. |
| 7 | "Your bank sent you this statement file. Check it against your records." | Bank screen, sentence at the end says whether books and bank agree | Every line answered; the sentence read out | Lines with no obvious answer (UPI settlements for restaurants). |
| 8 | "How much does your business owe, and how much is owed to you?" | Home tiles or Reports | Reads out the two right numbers | Which tile is which. |
| 9 | "Make a backup of your records." | Backups screen shows a done message | Backup made | Where backups are; jargon on the full tab (fixed, a plain screen now). |
| 10 | "Ask Total: how much did I sell this month?" (Ask Total box on Home) | An answer with the amount | Answer read out | Expecting a chat; phrases like "5000 Bluebird" were once not understood. |

### 4.2 Extra tasks by business type

Run these in place of task 10 if time is short; add them if time allows.

| Type | Extra task (read aloud) | End on | Done when | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Shop (kirana)** | "A customer brought back one item. Give the credit." (S) | Returns save card | Credit of the right amount, kept for the next bill | The words "credit" and "kept for the next bill". |
| **Shop (kirana)** | "You counted 12 packets on the shelf but the app says 15. Fix it." (S) | Stock count saved | Count saved, no confusion | Finding Stock count. |

| **Services** | "Send a client an invoice for one month's work, not yet paid." | Save card with the due line | Bill on credit | Choosing "Just an amount" instead of products. |
| **Manufacturer** | "You bought raw material with GST and paid some now. Record it." (G, S) | Purchase save card | GST claimed correctly | Opening products without a GST rate (now asked). |
| **Manufacturer** | "You made 20 packets from your raw material. Record it." (S) | Made goods saved | Stock of the finished product up | Finding Made goods; the recipe idea. |
| **Restaurant** | "Today's card and UPI sales came into the bank. Match them to your records." | Bank, UPI line answered | Line answered | A UPI settlement is answered as "other money in", a known wording gap. |
| **Restaurant** | "You bought a gas cylinder for cash. Record it." | Money out saved | Expense saved without a supplier | The supplier question on Record a purchase (known). |

**GST answer task (regular businesses only, replaces task 8):** "How much GST do you have to pay this month?" End on: Taxes. Done: they read the three figures aloud (sales tax, credit, to pay) and say which month. Watch for: last month showing 0 when this month's bills are the point.

## 6. Scoring and the pass line

- **A, done unaided:** they finished, with no hint, and the result is right.
- **B, done with a hint:** they finished after a level 1 or 2 hint, or after a long stall (over 2 minutes).
- **C, not done:** they gave up, or finished with a wrong result they did not notice (a bill with no GST that should have it counts as C).

**Suggested pass line for 1.0 (your call; the plan can be tightened):**

| Measure | Pass |
|---|---|
| Tasks 1 to 6 done unaided (A) | at least 80 percent of all attempts |
| Any single task | at least 3 of 5 per business type do it unaided |
| Wrong result they did not notice (C by wrong result) | zero for tax, zero for duplicate bills |
| Anyone who cannot finish setup (task 1) | none |
| Hints | no task needs a hint from more than 2 of 5 people |

Anything under the line becomes a fix before or right after 1.0, ranked by how many people stumbled.
After each batch of 5, count the "huh?" moments by screen. The screen with the most is fixed first.
