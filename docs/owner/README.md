# Owner documents

Written 15 October 2026 for the owner of Total. They describe the code as it stands on `main` at
commit `401b5d9a` (version 0.11.0 is the last tagged release). They are plain-English guides, not
developer documentation.

- `01-what-total-is.md` — what Total is, who it is for, the two modes, every feature, and what it
  cannot do yet. Ends with a glossary.
- `02-technical-progress.md` — how it is built and tested, what each phase delivered, what is open,
  what could be done next, and how to run it.
- `03-business-and-ideas.md` — customers, competitors, pricing options, go-to-market, risks, a
  product ideas backlog, the decisions you must take, and how to measure success.
- `04-plan-now-and-next.md` — your test-week checklist, the decisions with defaults, the launch
  gates, the 1.0.0 release steps and a roadmap to September 2027.
- `05-dependabot-triage.md` — the open security alerts and Dependabot PRs sorted by whether they reach users, with a recommendation for each group.
- `06-announcement-draft.md` — a DRAFT (nothing published) for the 1.0 announcement: WhatsApp, LinkedIn, a CA note and a 2-minute "first bill" video shot list.
- `07-beginner-study-kit.md` — everything for the real beginner study (D5): who to recruit, the script, the tasks, the note sheet, scoring and consent.

These files are for the owner. Review notes about them are kept outside the repository and are not
committed here.
